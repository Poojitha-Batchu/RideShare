About RideShare

RideShare is a modern ride-sharing platform that connects drivers and passengers traveling along similar routes. Users can offer rides, search rides, book seats, chat with drivers, and rate completed rides securely.

Key Features

Offer Ride

Create rides with pickup, drop, vehicle details, and seat availability.

Search Rides

Find rides based on pickup location, destination, and date.

Ride Chat

Passengers and drivers can communicate directly inside the app.

Ratings & Reviews

Users can rate completed rides and build trust in the community.

Multiple Payments

Supports UPI, Card, and Cash payment methods.

Ride Management

Manage bookings, offered rides, cancellations, and payment status.
